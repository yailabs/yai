# Project-control history

Authority: historical evidence and work records only.

`work/` preserves spines, waves, discovery, inventories, readiness notes, and
archives created while the repository evolved. Filenames such as
`current-status`, `roadmap`, `doctrine`, or `canonical` describe their original
project-control role; they no longer govern current YAI architecture or future
ordering.

Current authority lives in [`docs/index.md`](../docs/index.md). In particular,
[`docs/architecture.md`](../docs/architecture.md) owns executable truth and
[`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) owns implementation sequencing. Work records may
be cited to characterize behavior or explain provenance, but may not supersede
those documents.
